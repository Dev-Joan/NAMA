Brand & visual style
Clean, calm, reassuring "healthcare" feel — lots of the cream background as breathing room, soft rounded corners, generous padding, no clutter. Mirror the logo's soft, organic, slightly botanical warmth (the plant motif) rather than a clinical, sterile look.
Typography: an elegant, wide-tracked (letter-spaced) all-caps sans-serif for headings/logo-adjacent text (matching the "N A M A" spacing), paired with a clean, highly legible sans-serif for body copy. Use the bronze/gold color for small tracked-caps labels (eyebrow text above headings), the way the logo uses it for the sub-tagline.
Include simple fine-line (single-stroke, minimalist) illustrations in the olive-green brand color, in the same flat/simple style family as the logo's adult-and-child icon — no photos-only look. Specifically:
A fine-line drawing of a clinic building/facade for the hero section or about section.
Small fine-line icon illustrations representing each specialty: a speech bubble/mouth (orthophonie), a head with a brain/thought bubble (psychologie), a child in motion / balance figure (psychomotricité), and a plate with a leaf or apple (diététique).
Keep all line illustrations consistent in stroke weight and style so they read as one icon set.
Site should feel bilingual-ready: build primarily in French (since the specialties are named in French), with structure that makes it easy to add an English and/or Arabic toggle later.
